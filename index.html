Reading

Voting rights and election day

In the Russian Federation, citizens who have reached the age of 18 by voting day generally have the right to vote. Federal law also states that participation in elections is free and voluntary and is based on universal, equal and direct suffrage by secret ballot. These principles describe the legal framework; the detailed procedure depends on the type of election and the applicable rules.

At a polling station, a voter is typically identified and receives the necessary voting materials. A ballot is completed privately. The idea of a secret ballot is that other people should not know how an individual voted. Election officials organise the process and answer procedural questions, but the voter makes the decision independently.

Election vocabulary is useful beyond politics. Expressions such as be eligible to vote, cast a vote, voter turnout, polling station and election results frequently appear in news reports, academic discussions and legal English.

Legal basis used for this neutral language-practice text: Federal Law No. 67-FZ on basic guarantees of electoral rights; Federal Law No. 20-FZ for State Duma elections.
